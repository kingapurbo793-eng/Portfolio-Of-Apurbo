About Me

Building ideas into
real digital experiences.

I'm Apurbo Roy Biswajit, a web developer, designer, and dedicated student currently studying at Tejia Government High School. What started as curiosity about how websites work has grown into a genuine passion for crafting clean, functional, and visually compelling digital experiences.

I spend my time learning modern front-end technologies, experimenting with design systems, and building projects that push my skills a little further each time. I believe great web design sits at the intersection of clarity, creativity, and code — and that's exactly the space I love working in.

Every project I take on is an opportunity to learn something new, refine my craft, and move one step closer to becoming the developer and designer I'm working hard to be.
